making soundfont babies?

babymaker demo

Three instruments sit on the dots. The pink dot is the playhead: wherever you drag it, the note Mozart is playing is rebuilt from the three at the weights the lines show. Click a dot to swap its instrument; drag the dots around too. This is not a crossfade: the attack, the formants, the harmonics and the decay each land in between.

how it works

Every note is taken apart in the MCLT domain into a level per frame, a spectral envelope (Röbel and Rodet's true envelope), the fine structure under it, and time. The sources are aligned with DTW on level and low-order cepstrum, so a slow and a fast attack interpolate in length. Level and envelope are averaged in dB and the fine structure in |a|0.6. The waveform comes from the recordings: each source is WSOLA-warped onto the shared timeline, the warped sources are summed with the weights, the mix's magnitudes are swapped for the model's, and two Griffin-Lim passes make them agree.

This page runs the TypeScript port in a Web Worker, with env_mode="log". The Python version also morphs the formant band as an optimal-transport barycentre. See the repository's README for the details.
